About Me

From Bartender to
Strategic Ops Leader

I built my career from the ground up — literally starting as a bartender and growing into a cross-functional executive leading multi-site operations, digital transformation initiatives, and AI-powered workflows. That journey shaped how I think: with both a systems perspective and a people-first lens.

With over a decade of progressive leadership, I've delivered complex projects end-to-end — from implementing enterprise inventory systems across five restaurant locations to founding and scaling my own cocktail catering company. I thrive at the intersection of operations, technology, and team development, bringing strategic clarity and hands-on execution together.

What drives me is creating systems that actually work — ones that reduce friction, scale with growth, and empower the people using them. I'm actively integrating Generative AI into my workflows and love finding smart ways to make teams operate at their best.
